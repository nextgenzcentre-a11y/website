## Content and technical constraints

- The React programme finder, Firebase editable fields, application portals, DNS and email infrastructure must be preserved.
- Existing programme finder: 1,182 programme listings at 25 universities; this is a reference dataset, not a live official admissions feed. One university or programme is **not** a confirmed partner solely because it appears in the finder.
- Public programme directories: 25 university pages, 6 study-level pages, 53 programme details and 14 editorial subject comparisons plus a course directory for seven English language centres.
- All public prices and intake dates should carry a source/basis; do not imply the finder price is a final current university quotation.
- Official UCSI Foundation in Arts international estimate differs materially from legacy finder data. The public pages should always show the university estimate and warn about the older number.
- Google Search Console still shows the homepage indexed, while most newly generated programme pages are unknown/discovered. Technical crawlability and sitemap inclusion do **not** equal Google indexing.
- The site currently links to its 114-URL sitemap: https://www.nextgenzcentre.space/sitemap.xml. Google may crawl and index on its own schedule, without any guarantee.
- Prioritize original detailed comparisons, official programme sources, credible university partner links, and conversion-quality content. Do not promise first-page placements.
- Reassess GSC query-level impressions and indexed pages before updating priorities. Google Search Console data has a usual lag of several days.
