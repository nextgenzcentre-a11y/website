Which English programme is right for an international student?

General English typically focuses on language development, while intensive English may involve more scheduled class time. IELTS preparation focuses on assessment preparation; it is not automatically an accepted university admission pathway. Ask about level placement, class duration, expected outcomes, class hours, availability and whether the specific centre supports an eligible student pass route.

Students planning to progress to a university should check the chosen university’s language requirements rather than assuming any centre course guarantees entry. Read our English courses guide and EMGS student visa overview.
