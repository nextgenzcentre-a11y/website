Pharmacy programmes may include pharmaceutical chemistry, pharmacology, therapeutics, laboratory practice and professional placements. Exact recognition is essential for anyone intending to practise as a pharmacist.

Frequently asked questions

Will a pharmacy degree qualify me to practise in any country?

No. Pharmacist registration depends on local professional rules and qualification recognition.

Are all listed bachelor's options currently accepting international students?

No. The listed titles are reference entries. Each university must confirm its current admissions status, recognised entry qualifications, course language, campus, fee schedule and visa-related eligibility. Your nationality and chosen study mode may affect the applicable student-pass route.
