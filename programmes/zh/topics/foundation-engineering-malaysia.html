What should you know about Foundation in Engineering Malaysia?

Foundation in Engineering programmes may help students prepare for undergraduate engineering study, but the relationship between a foundation and an engineering degree is not always automatic. Entry rules, mathematics or physics requirements, assessment and progression policies depend on the institution and the degree pursued next.

How to choose the right Foundation in Engineering Malaysia option

The following are specific checks to make before submitting an application or paying a deposit. When comparing institutions, ask the admissions team to confirm the answers in writing.

Target engineering discipline

Check progression to mechanical, electrical, civil, chemical or any other specific engineering award before enrolment.

Mathematics and physics

Look for the actual foundation syllabus and prerequisites rather than assuming every engineering foundation has identical subjects.

University progression route

Ask for the institution's current written policy on minimum grades needed to progress to the chosen degree.

Fees and intake

Verify the academic start date, actual campus and current total foundation costs, including registration and laboratory charges.

Frequently asked questions

Does Foundation in Engineering guarantee a Bachelor of Engineering place?

No. Progression may depend on passing the foundation and meeting programme-specific requirements.

Can I change engineering discipline after the foundation?

That depends on the foundation syllabus and the destination degree's subject requirements. Ask the institution before deciding.

Applying to university in Malaysia

Start by confirming your latest qualification, desired study intake, budget, nationality and English evidence. Ask each university to verify the exact programme and current admissions requirements. After an appropriate offer, follow the provider and official EMGS instructions for any applicable international student-pass process.
