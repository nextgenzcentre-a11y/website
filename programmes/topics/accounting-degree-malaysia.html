What should you know about Bachelor of Accounting Malaysia?

A Bachelor of Accounting, a Bachelor of Accountancy and an Accounting and Finance degree may have different module combinations. Students considering a later professional qualification should check the specific programme's exemptions, if any, with the relevant professional accountancy organisation. Do not infer professional recognition solely from a course name.

How to choose the right Bachelor of Accounting Malaysia option

The following are specific checks to make before submitting an application or paying a deposit. When comparing institutions, ask the admissions team to confirm the answers in writing.

Programme modules

Compare auditing, taxation, corporate reporting, management accounting, analytics and practical case work.

Professional exemptions

Confirm any accreditation or exemptions with the relevant professional body for the exact university course and study intake.

Internship or practice

Ask whether practical placements or workplace experience are included or required.

International admission

Check academic prerequisites, English proficiency and the full international tuition schedule.

Frequently asked questions

Does an accounting degree in Malaysia automatically qualify me as an accountant abroad?

No. Professional registration may require separate exams and work experience in the relevant country.

What is the difference between Accounting and Accounting & Finance?

Accounting and Finance programmes may include additional investment and corporate finance modules. Confirm the published curriculum.

How should I compare tuition for accounting degrees?

Request a current written international fee schedule; many reference figures have different semester, year and total bases.

Applying to university in Malaysia

Start by confirming your latest qualification, desired study intake, budget, nationality and English evidence. Ask each university to verify the exact programme and current admissions requirements. After an appropriate offer, follow the provider and official EMGS instructions for any applicable international student-pass process.
