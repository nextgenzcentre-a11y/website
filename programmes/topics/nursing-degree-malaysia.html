What should you know about Nursing Degree Malaysia?

Not all nursing qualifications or delivery formats have the same career implications. Some finder entries relate to a bachelor's nursing degree, while other institutions list diplomas or advanced nursing routes separately. If you plan to register as a nurse in Malaysia or another country, confirm whether the specific award meets the relevant professional body's requirements.

Ask our admissions team about programme availability, entry requirements and fees for your intake.

How to choose the right Nursing Degree Malaysia option

The following are specific checks to make before submitting an application or paying a deposit. When comparing institutions, ask the admissions team to confirm the answers in writing.

Clinical training

Ask how practice placements are arranged, where they happen and whether international students may participate in required clinical activities.

Professional recognition

Check university accreditation and nursing registration rules in the country where you intend to practise.

Degree title

Differentiate a Bachelor of Nursing, Bachelor of Nursing Science and other related titles; read the official academic programme description.

Entry and health checks

Confirm science prerequisites, language evidence and any medical, vaccination or other clinical-placement checks required.

Frequently asked questions

Does a Malaysian nursing degree automatically let me work abroad?

No. Professional licensing and qualification recognition are controlled by the relevant country's nursing authority.

What is the difference between a diploma and bachelor's nursing route?

They have different qualification levels and may lead to different progression or registration requirements. Verify the programme and professional rules.

Do international nursing students need English test scores?

Language requirements depend on the programme and clinical conditions. Ask the university for its current accepted English evidence.

Applying to university in Malaysia

Start by confirming your latest qualification, desired study intake, budget, nationality and English evidence. Ask each university to verify the exact programme and current admissions requirements. After an appropriate offer, follow the provider and official EMGS instructions for any applicable international student-pass process.
