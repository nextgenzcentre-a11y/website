What should you know about Foundation in Arts Malaysia?

The term Foundation in Arts does not necessarily mean fine arts. Depending on the institution, it may be a broader academic pre-university pathway. Students intending to pursue business, communication, education, social sciences or other degrees should ask which outcomes the precise foundation qualification supports.

How to choose the right Foundation in Arts Malaysia option

The following are specific checks to make before submitting an application or paying a deposit. When comparing institutions, ask the admissions team to confirm the answers in writing.

Progression

Get a written list of bachelor’s degrees for which the foundation is accepted and the minimum grades required.

Subjects and assessment

Compare the syllabus, language requirements, general subjects and any available field-specific components.

Future flexibility

Ask whether the foundation can be used at another university or only within the institution offering it.

Total cost

Verify the current international foundation tuition and any extra administrative or student-pass costs.

Frequently asked questions

Is Foundation in Arts only for fine art degrees?

No. The exact pathway depends on the institution. Check its official progression routes.

Can Foundation in Arts lead to a business bachelor's degree?

It may at some institutions, subject to the destination degree's requirements. Confirm directly with the chosen university.

Is a foundation the same as a diploma?

No. They generally serve different academic purposes and may have different qualification levels and progression rules.

Applying to university in Malaysia

Start by confirming your latest qualification, desired study intake, budget, nationality and English evidence. Ask each university to verify the exact programme and current admissions requirements. After an appropriate offer, follow the provider and official EMGS instructions for any applicable international student-pass process.
