Current information from Multimedia University (MMU)

Duration and language
MMU lists a one-year foundation delivered entirely in English.
Entry requirements
For the SPM or O-Level route, MMU describes five Grade C credits including English, Mathematics and an engineering-related subject, or accepted equivalents.
Progression
MMU describes degree pathways in its Faculty of Artificial Intelligence and Engineering and Faculty of Engineering and Technology after the foundation.
Study content
The university's listed modules include mathematics, mechanics, physics, engineering-related concepts and academic English across trimesters.

MMU's international fee schedule includes promotional and previous prices that can differ from the legacy NextGenz finder amount. Request a written 2026/2027 international quote, including dates and eligibility.
