Is this programme right for you?

A foundation pathway may prepare students for a later degree. Ask the university which degrees accept the foundation qualification and whether progression is conditional.

For engineering courses, verify the exact discipline, laboratory or industry-training requirements and relevant programme accreditation.

Questions to ask before you apply

  1. Is Foundation in Engineering still accepting international applicants for the next intake at Universiti Tenaga Nasional (UNITEN)?
  2. What grades, prior qualifications and English-language evidence are required for this exact programme?
  3. Which campus delivers the programme, what is the teaching mode, and what is the official award title?
  4. Is the current quoted tuition per semester, academic year, or entire qualification? What additional deposits and official fees apply?
  5. Which steps must be completed before a student visa application or entry to Malaysia?

How to apply in Malaysia

Compare eligibility and budget first, then collect the documents stated in the university’s current instructions. Review any written offer and payment schedule before committing. Visa and student pass procedures depend on your nationality and study level; use the official EMGS website for current information.
