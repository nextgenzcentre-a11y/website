For undergraduate applications, review academic prerequisites, course-specific language requirements and the duration and location of the actual degree programme.

For a healthcare-related course, ask about clinical placements and any professional registration or recognition that applies in your home country.

Before applying as an international student

  1. Check your route. Confirm any subject prerequisites, where practical or professional placements take place, and who arranges them if the course includes one.
  2. Match your offer to the course. For Bachelor of Nursing Science, ask Universiti Malaya (UM) to confirm the exact award, campus, study mode and start date.
  3. Confirm your total before paying. Ask for the tuition basis and a written breakdown of extra charges for your intake.
