What does Mechanical Engineering study involve?

Mechanical engineering courses can include mechanics, materials, thermodynamics, fluid systems and design. Distinguish conventional engineering awards from engineering technology qualifications.

What to know about this qualification level

Diplomas have their own award level and often differ from preparatory foundation programmes. Ask whether the specific diploma provides relevant practical training or credit transfer toward a later degree.

Frequently asked questions

Are all mechanical engineering degrees equivalent for professional registration?

No. The exact award, accreditation, delivery mode and relevant professional rules matter.

Are all listed diploma options currently accepting international students?

No. The listed titles are reference entries. Each university must confirm its current admissions status, recognised entry qualifications, course language, campus, fee schedule and visa-related eligibility. Your nationality and chosen study mode may affect the applicable student-pass route.
