What does Business Administration study involve?

Business administration courses may cover marketing, finance, operations, leadership, strategy and organisational decision-making. At postgraduate level, an MBA has different entry expectations from an undergraduate business degree.

What to know about this qualification level

Bachelor's degrees vary in professional recognition, study duration, curriculum and campus delivery. A broad subject title does not guarantee the same modules or award across universities.

How to compare business administration options

Programme stage

Confirm whether you need an undergraduate business degree, a specialised master's degree or an MBA route.

Specialisation and cases

Compare accounting, finance, HR, leadership and applied case-study modules.

Experience expectations

Ask about professional experience and project requirements, particularly for postgraduate study.

Questions for international applicants

Pre-requisites

Which school, foundation or diploma qualifications and English evidence are accepted?

Qualification recognition

What is the exact named award and its current recognition for the student's intended future use?

Duration and total fee

How long is the degree, which campus hosts it, and how is international tuition billed?

Frequently asked questions

Is a bachelor's in business administration the same as an MBA?

No. Bachelor's and MBA qualifications are different levels, with different entry requirements and academic objectives.

Are all listed bachelor's options currently accepting international students?

No. The listed titles are reference entries. Each university must confirm its current admissions status, recognised entry qualifications, course language, campus, fee schedule and visa-related eligibility. Your nationality and chosen study mode may affect the applicable student-pass route.

Yes. NextGenz Centre offers free core university-placement guidance. Tell us your latest qualification, target field, budget and preferred intake. University, medical, immigration and other third-party charges remain separate; no admission or visa approval is guaranteed.
