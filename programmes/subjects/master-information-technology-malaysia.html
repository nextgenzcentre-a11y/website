What does Information Technology study involve?

Information technology often concentrates on the use, management and integration of information systems. Students should distinguish IT from Computer Science, software development and information security before deciding on a qualification.

What to know about this qualification level

Master's programmes may follow taught coursework, research or mixed modes and may require prior degrees in specific subjects, a research proposal or professional experience.

How to compare information technology options

Programme focus

Compare networking, databases, cloud infrastructure, systems administration and business information systems modules.

Technical versus management content

Ask whether the programme focuses on technology implementation, software development or organisational IT.

Hands-on study

Review assessment projects and practical placement requirements when available.

Questions for international applicants

Entry background

Is an undergraduate degree in a related subject necessary, and is professional experience required?

Taught or research

How many taught modules are included, and what dissertation, project or thesis is required?

Visa and format

Does the actual study mode support an international student pass where necessary?

Frequently asked questions

Is an information technology qualification equivalent to computer science?

Not automatically. Universities award different qualifications and their curricula can differ significantly. Check the subject modules and recognised award.

Are all listed master's options currently accepting international students?

No. The listed titles are reference entries. Each university must confirm its current admissions status, recognised entry qualifications, course language, campus, fee schedule and visa-related eligibility. Your nationality and chosen study mode may affect the applicable student-pass route.
