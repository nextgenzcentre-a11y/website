What does Civil Engineering study involve?

Civil engineering programmes may focus on structures, geotechnics, construction, transportation, water or environmental systems. Programme recognition and practical learning are important when selecting a course.

What to know about this qualification level

Bachelor's degrees vary in professional recognition, study duration, curriculum and campus delivery. A broad subject title does not guarantee the same modules or award across universities.
