What does Computer Science study involve?

Computer science can involve programming, algorithms, mathematics, databases, computing systems and optional specialisations. A broadly named Computer Science degree and one focused on networks, multimedia or security may teach different modules.

What to know about this qualification level

Master's programmes may follow taught coursework, research or mixed modes and may require prior degrees in specific subjects, a research proposal or professional experience.

Compare master’s Computer Science options in Malaysia

For international applicants, one of the most important differences is whether the programme is on campus, mixed-mode or open and distance learning (ODL). These are not interchangeable for attendance and immigration purposes. We checked the universities’ own current course descriptions rather than relying only on older finder titles.

Multimedia University (MMU): Master in Computer Science – ODL

MMU's current programme page calls the award Master in Computer Science – ODL (By Coursework), offered through its Faculty of Computing and Informatics. The existing NextGenz finder uses the older title “Master of Computer Science in Software Engineering (ODL)”. MMU describes software engineering and broader computing electives; check the university's current specialisations, admission rules and fees before applying.

Universiti Kuala Lumpur (UniKL): Master in Computer Science

UniKL publishes mixed-mode and ODL programme variants. Its official ODL listing describes a 1.5-year full-time programme, March and October intakes, and a published international tuition reference of MYR 27,090 for that ODL variant. This must not be confused with the older MYR 16,000 reference associated with the general mixed-mode finder listing. Ask UniKL for a current quote for your intended variant and study mode. More

Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM): Master of Computer Science – ODL

UTM's admission page describes an online, mixed-mode Master of Computer Science involving 45 credits, with a published duration of approximately 1.5–4 years. Its official international fee figures depend on the applicable programme and fee schedule; do not rely on an undated finder price. UTM also publishes conditions for international students on ODL programmes, including relevant EMGS study approval requirements.

Can I apply for a master's Computer Science programme without a bachelor's in computing?

Some universities may admit related degree holders subject to additional computing prerequisites or bridging courses. UTM, for example, describes added subjects for certain non-Computer-Science degree holders. You must check your exact background against the university's current course-specific admissions rules.

Is an online Malaysian master's the same as studying on campus?

No. ODL can be suitable for remote learning but attendance, visa and study-approval requirements may be different. The university and official authorities—not an education consultant—decide those rules. If you intend to live in Malaysia, confirm the eligible study mode before paying a deposit.

Which other Master's programmes are similar?

Students interested in closely related fields can also compare Master's in Software Engineering, Master's in Data Science, and Master's in Cybersecurity. These are distinct awards: confirm the curriculum, exact qualification and university conditions.

How to compare computer science options

Curriculum depth

Review algorithms, programming, mathematics and practical projects in the actual module list rather than judging the subject from the course title.

Specialisation and award

Check whether the university awards Computer Science, Computer Science with an emphasis, or a separate Information Technology qualification.

Practical experience

Compare capstone projects, industrial placements, computing facilities and any specialist laboratory requirements.

Questions for international applicants

Entry background

Is an undergraduate degree in a related subject necessary, and is professional experience required?

Taught or research

How many taught modules are included, and what dissertation, project or thesis is required?

Visa and format

Does the actual study mode support an international student pass where necessary?

Frequently asked questions

Can a computer science programme include software engineering or data science?

It may include related modules, but that is not the same as obtaining an independently named specialist award. Check the actual modules and qualification title.

Are all listed master's options currently accepting international students?

No. The listed titles are reference entries. Each university must confirm its current admissions status, recognised entry qualifications, course language, campus, fee schedule and visa-related eligibility. Your nationality and chosen study mode may affect the applicable student-pass route.
