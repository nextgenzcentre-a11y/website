What does Data Science and Analytics study involve?

Data science and analytics programmes blend statistics, data handling, programming and quantitative methods in different proportions. Some courses emphasise business analytics while others emphasise machine learning and technical research.

How to compare data science and analytics options

Prerequisite skills

Check mathematics, statistics and programming entry requirements, particularly for postgraduate courses.

Taught content

Compare machine learning, data engineering, visualisation, databases and quantitative methods in the curriculum.

Research versus applied projects

Ask whether the course includes an independent thesis, business capstone or placement.

Questions for international applicants

Pre-requisites

Which school, foundation or diploma qualifications and English evidence are accepted?

Qualification recognition

What is the exact named award and its current recognition for the student's intended future use?

Duration and total fee

How long is the degree, which campus hosts it, and how is international tuition billed?

Frequently asked questions

Is data science the same as business analytics?

They overlap, but analytics programmes may focus more on business decisions while data science can emphasise computing and statistics. Compare actual course modules.

Are all listed bachelor's options currently accepting international students?

No. The listed titles are reference entries. Each university must confirm its current admissions status, recognised entry qualifications, course language, campus, fee schedule and visa-related eligibility. Your nationality and chosen study mode may affect the applicable student-pass route.
