What does Education and Teaching study involve?

Education programmes can focus on primary, secondary, language teaching, leadership, educational technology or research. An education degree is not necessarily a teaching licence.

What to know about this qualification level

Bachelor's degrees vary in professional recognition, study duration, curriculum and campus delivery. A broad subject title does not guarantee the same modules or award across universities.

Source and availability: Programme names, university locations, fee references and links below come from the NextGenz Centre existing finder database, not a live university admissions feed. A listed title does not mean that an intake is currently open or that NextGenz has an official relationship with that university. Current fees, entry requirements, course mode and availability must be checked in the university’s written information. Finder fees can differ in billing basis, and should never be treated as final quotes.

Frequently asked questions

Does an education degree guarantee a teaching licence?

Not in every country. Registration, practical teaching requirements and qualification recognition vary by jurisdiction.

Are all listed bachelor's options currently accepting international students?

No. The listed titles are reference entries. Each university must confirm its current admissions status, recognised entry qualifications, course language, campus, fee schedule and visa-related eligibility. Your nationality and chosen study mode may affect the applicable student-pass route.

Can I ask NextGenz Centre to help me compare the universities?

Yes. NextGenz Centre offers free core university-placement guidance. Tell us your latest qualification, target field, budget and preferred intake. University, medical, immigration and other third-party charges remain separate; no admission or visa approval is guaranteed.
