What does Medicine study involve?

Medical study involves regulated professional pathways and clinical placements. Programmes using MBBS, Doctor of Medicine or other titles must be evaluated against the exact award, curriculum and recognition rules.

What to know about this qualification level

Master's programmes may follow taught coursework, research or mixed modes and may require prior degrees in specific subjects, a research proposal or professional experience.

How to compare medicine options

Professional eligibility

Check the specific qualification and its recognition by medical authorities where you intend to practise.

Clinical training

Confirm clinical placement arrangements, language requirements and programme duration directly with the university.

Academic admission

Review science prerequisites, admissions assessments and any country-specific conditions.

Questions for international applicants

Entry background

Is an undergraduate degree in a related subject necessary, and is professional experience required?

Taught or research

How many taught modules are included, and what dissertation, project or thesis is required?

Visa and format

Does the actual study mode support an international student pass where necessary?

Frequently asked questions

Does a medical degree automatically let me practise in another country?

No. Licensing, exams, internship and qualification recognition may be separately required.

Are all listed master's options currently accepting international students?

No. The listed titles are reference entries. Each university must confirm its current admissions status, recognised entry qualifications, course language, campus, fee schedule and visa-related eligibility. Your nationality and chosen study mode may affect the applicable student-pass route.

Can I ask NextGenz Centre to help me compare the universities?

Yes. NextGenz Centre offers free core university-placement guidance. Tell us your latest qualification, target field, budget and preferred intake. University, medical, immigration and other third-party charges remain separate; no admission or visa approval is guaranteed.
