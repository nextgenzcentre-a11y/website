What does Software Engineering study involve?

Software engineering programmes can emphasise requirements analysis, code quality, testing, architecture and the software delivery lifecycle. Some universities place software engineering within a broader computer science programme.

What to know about this qualification level

Master's programmes may follow taught coursework, research or mixed modes and may require prior degrees in specific subjects, a research proposal or professional experience.

How to compare software engineering options

Award type

Confirm whether the final award is Software Engineering or Computer Science with a software focus.

Development lifecycle

Compare system design, secure development, testing, teamwork, DevOps and capstone requirements.

Industry relevance

Ask about real projects, company placements and opportunities to build a practical portfolio.

Questions for international applicants

Entry background

Is an undergraduate degree in a related subject necessary, and is professional experience required?

Taught or research

How many taught modules are included, and what dissertation, project or thesis is required?

Visa and format

Does the actual study mode support an international student pass where necessary?

Frequently asked questions

Does a software engineering course always teach the same programming languages?

No. Programming tools and modules vary by university and intake. Review the latest official handbook.

Are all listed master's options currently accepting international students?

No. The listed titles are reference entries. Each university must confirm its current admissions status, recognised entry qualifications, course language, campus, fee schedule and visa-related eligibility. Your nationality and chosen study mode may affect the applicable student-pass route.
