What does Biotechnology study involve?

Biotechnology and biomedical science degrees can include molecular biology, genetics, laboratory research and industrial applications. Their programme focuses and professional recognition may differ.

Frequently asked questions

Is biotechnology the same as biomedical science?

Not necessarily. Biotechnology may emphasise applications across sectors, while biomedical sciences can focus on human disease and health research.

Are all listed bachelor's options currently accepting international students?

No. The listed titles are reference entries. Each university must confirm its current admissions status, recognised entry qualifications, course language, campus, fee schedule and visa-related eligibility. Your nationality and chosen study mode may affect the applicable student-pass route.
