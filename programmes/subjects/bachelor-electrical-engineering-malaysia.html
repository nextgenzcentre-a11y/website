What does Electrical and Electronic Engineering study involve?

Electrical and electronic engineering studies may involve circuits, signal processing, control, telecommunications or power systems. A degree's specialisation and accreditation affect progression and career pathways.

What to know about this qualification level

Bachelor's degrees vary in professional recognition, study duration, curriculum and campus delivery. A broad subject title does not guarantee the same modules or award across universities.

Frequently asked questions

Is electrical engineering the same as electronic engineering?

They overlap, but individual programmes can focus on different technologies and applications.

Are all listed bachelor's options currently accepting international students?

No. The listed titles are reference entries. Each university must confirm its current admissions status, recognised entry qualifications, course language, campus, fee schedule and visa-related eligibility. Your nationality and chosen study mode may affect the applicable student-pass route.
