A practical choice for international students

Six reasons students choose Malaysia

Quality education matters first. The city, daily budget, language, campus setting and opportunities beyond classes also shape a successful study experience. Start with these practical advantages, then verify the details for your specific course.

01

Study options for different goals

Explore English preparation, foundation, diploma, bachelor’s and postgraduate routes. Compare the actual programme, faculty and award—not only the university’s name.

02

Education with quality checks

Malaysia’s Qualifications Agency maintains the Malaysian Qualifications Register. Check the accreditation status of the exact qualification before applying.

03

English-medium study options

Many institutions offer courses taught in English. Teaching language, IELTS requirements and accepted English preparation vary by programme, so confirm each course’s rules.

04

Manageable living choices

Accommodation, meals and transport can be planned around a budget. Compare Kuala Lumpur, Selangor, Penang and other locations instead of assuming one fixed national cost.

05

Multicultural everyday life

Malay, Chinese, Indian and many other communities shape Malaysia’s food, languages and celebrations. Students can explore different cultural traditions while studying.

06

A full student experience

After class, discover city parks, local markets, beaches, museums and highland attractions. Trips and activities are optional lifestyle experiences—not study programme benefits.

For independent guidance on studying here, see Education Malaysia Global Services’ reasons to study in Malaysia.

Education comes first

Find the right degree, then the right place to live

Start with the subject you want, the academic entry requirements and the campus where it is actually taught. Foundation programmes can prepare students for a related degree; diplomas may offer a more practical route; bachelor’s and master’s programmes have different requirements and progression options.

Our programme directory helps you compare Malaysian universities, and the bachelor’s guide is useful if you already know your study level. University rankings are one reference, not a substitute for checking the curriculum, accreditation and career relevance of your course.

  • Check the course’s teaching language, intake and academic requirements.
  • Verify the qualification through the official MQA register.
  • Confirm the campus, daily commute, accommodation and written tuition quotation.
  • Review EMGS student-pass steps before making travel plans.

Fun near the Klang Valley

Sunway Lagoon and the Sunway City area

In Bandar Sunway, students can find a well-known leisure district with shopping, dining and the Sunway Lagoon theme park. The water attractions and park are entertainment options rather than university services.

The appeal is practical: a day out with friends can fit into a study-life schedule without needing a flight to another part of Malaysia. Always check current tickets, opening hours, transport, weather and any park rules with the operator.

Read about the park at the official Sunway Group leisure page.

Cooler air above the city

Genting Highlands — mountain views and entertainment

Genting Highlands is a popular hilltop destination in Pahang near the Klang Valley. It is known for its higher-altitude climate, resort areas and cable car journeys over forested slopes.

For students, the attraction can be a change of scenery: a day trip or weekend outing to see the mountains and enjoy shopping or leisure facilities. Visibility may be limited by cloud or rain, and schedules can change.

Choose transport based on your actual campus location, not just a map distance from central Kuala Lumpur.

Nature beyond the cities

Islands, mountains and green tea country

Students who love nature can discover quite different parts of Malaysia. A Langkawi beach weekend, Cameron Highlands tea landscapes and other regional trips require different travel times and budgets.

Langkawi islands

Langkawi in Kedah is known for beaches, island scenery and outdoor activities. Travel and accommodation expenses depend on season and itinerary.

Langkawi coastlines

The island’s bays, hills and coastal scenery offer a different view from city life. Plan water activities with licensed operators.

Cameron Highlands

Tea plantations and a cooler mountain climate make Cameron Highlands a popular highland escape. Weather and road travel affect planning.

Highland tea landscapes

Green tea hills, farm visits and walking viewpoints can be part of a relaxed mountain outing.

Kedah coastal scenery

Forested coastlines, islands and sea views make the north-western coast attractive for nature lovers.

George Town heritage streets

Historic shophouses, local businesses and cultural neighbourhoods show the everyday heritage of Penang.
