Privacy & Cookie Notice

Last updated: 22 November 2026

NEXTGENZ CENTRE is an education consultancy in Malaysia. This notice describes information handled through our public website, programme enquiries, and Student and Agent Portal access requests.

Information you provide

When you contact us or request portal access, we may collect your name, email address, WhatsApp number, programme interest and the type of portal requested. Agents may also provide their agency name. Other information may be requested later during an admissions process, depending on the service you choose.

You do not need to provide a passport number or upload identity documents just to request Student Portal access.

How we use information

We use enquiry details to respond to you, assess portal access requests, provide education consultancy and admissions support, send relevant follow-ups, and maintain our services. Portal requests are stored in our portal database and may be included in admissions email notifications.

Service providers and third-party links

Our website uses services such as Firebase for parts of the portal functionality, email delivery services for relevant notifications, and Google Translate for optional language functionality. Some pages link to third-party university websites, news articles, WhatsApp and other services. Those providers have their own privacy policies and may process information when you use their services.

Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies or browser storage may be used for necessary site and portal functions, sign-in state, preferences, or requested translation features. Your browser settings can help you manage or delete cookies, although disabling necessary storage may affect some functions.

Google advertising

Status: As of this notice's update, NEXTGENZ CENTRE is applying for Google AdSense site approval. Ads are not yet active simply because the website has been verified.

If advertisements are enabled on this website in the future, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or other identifiers to select, measure, or personalise ads as permitted by law and your applicable consent choices. Users can find out how Google uses information on sites using its services at Google's partner-site privacy information, and manage personalised advertising through My Ad Center.

Where required, we will provide consent controls for advertising technologies before activating them. We do not ask visitors to click advertisements or reward them for doing so.

Security, retention, and your choices

We take reasonable measures to protect information we handle and retain it for the purposes described here, consistent with applicable legal, operational, and recordkeeping requirements. You may ask about your information and request access, correction, or deletion where applicable. Some records may need to be retained to meet legal obligations.

Contact us

For privacy questions or requests relating to your information, email Admission@nextgenzcentre.space. You may also contact us through the official contact options on our website.
