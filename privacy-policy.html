NextGenz Centre

Portal privacy notice

This notice covers requests for access to the NextGenz Centre Student and Agent Portals.

What the request collects

We collect your name, email address, WhatsApp number and the type of portal you request. If you arrive from a programme page, the request may include your programme interest. Agents can provide their agency name.

You do not need to provide your passport number or upload documents to request student access.

How the request is used

NextGenz Centre uses the information to review your request, contact you about portal access and follow up on your study or agency enquiry. Requests are stored in the portal database and the details are included in an email notification for admissions.

Questions about your information

For questions about your request or to ask to access, correct or delete the information you provided, email Admission@nextgenzcentre.space.
