How to Study in Malaysia: A Step-by-Step Guide for International Students

From choosing a university to preparing documents and planning your arrival.

Published 13 November 2026 · By NextGenz Centre Editorial Team

Planning to study in Malaysia? The best starting point is not an application form. It is a shortlist of programmes that match your academic history, budget, preferred city and future career goals. This practical guide walks international students through the decisions that matter.

1. Choose your level and field of study

Decide whether you are looking for a foundation, diploma, bachelor's degree, master's degree, PhD or English language course. Compare the actual programme curriculum, duration, teaching language, campus location, accreditation and entry requirements rather than choosing a university based on its name alone.

Ask each institution for the latest programme brochure and a written fee schedule. Tuition may be displayed per semester, per year or for the full programme; these figures are not interchangeable.

2. Check eligibility before applying

Universities may ask for academic certificates, transcripts, evidence of English proficiency and programme-specific documents. Some courses have interviews, portfolios or prerequisite subjects. A conditional offer is not the same as final admission.

  • Prepare clear copies of your passport's biodata page and academic records.
  • Keep course names, university names and intake dates consistent across your application.
  • Check whether your certificates need translations or verification.
  • Confirm any English-language test or exemption directly with the university.

3. Compare your complete budget

Include tuition, registration and administrative fees, applicable taxes, accommodation, transport, meals, health insurance and visa-related fees. Also make room for a deposit and unexpected expenses. Ask what must be paid before the visa application, after approval and at enrolment.

If you are offered a scholarship, request its written terms. The percentage, duration, eligible fees and renewal conditions can make a major difference.

4. Submit the application and review the offer

When your documents are ready, apply to the institution and wait for its admission decision. Before accepting an offer, check the exact programme, campus, intake, tuition structure, refund policy and any conditions that remain to be satisfied.

5. Understand the student pass process

International students generally need to follow the applicable student pass process. Education Malaysia Global Services (EMGS) explains the stages from offer letter to application, assessment, eVAL approval and post-arrival requirements. Requirements vary by nationality and institution, so avoid making travel arrangements based on assumptions.

Read our Malaysia student visa and EMGS guide before planning flights.

6. Prepare to arrive and settle in

After receiving the required approvals, confirm the relevant entry-visa rules and arrival instructions. Arrange accommodation, organise your documents and contact the university about orientation, medical screening and registration. Keep digital and printed copies of important approvals.
