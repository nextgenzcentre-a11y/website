Scholarships in Malaysia for International Students: What to Check

Understand partial scholarships, eligibility and renewal conditions.

Published 13 November 2026 · By NextGenz Centre Editorial Team

A scholarship can make an education more affordable, but the word “scholarship” covers different kinds of awards. Some are competitive grants; others are partial tuition discounts offered by an institution. Before making a study decision, understand what the award truly pays for.

Full awards versus partial discounts

A full award may cover tuition and sometimes additional costs, but these are not available for every course or applicant. A partial scholarship usually reduces only an eligible part of the tuition bill. It may exclude application, registration, visa, accommodation and living expenses.

NextGenz Centre can help students explore suitable partial scholarship opportunities where available; we do not promise full scholarships or guaranteed awards.

What affects eligibility?

Institutional criteria may include academic grades, programme, nationality, intake, extracurricular achievements and available funding. Competitive awards can have their own applications and deadlines. A programme might not participate in the same scheme as another programme at the same university.

Ask these five questions

  1. Which exact fees does the award cover?
  2. Is the percentage applied once, per semester or throughout the programme?
  3. What academic performance is required to retain the award?
  4. Can it be combined with another discount?
  5. When and how will the award be confirmed in writing?

Compare the net cost, not the headline percentage

A larger advertised discount on a more expensive degree may still leave higher total tuition than a smaller discount on another programme. Compare the tuition after the award, programme length and additional charges.

Prepare your application early

Keep academic certificates, transcripts and supporting evidence organised. Ask about award deadlines separately from general admission deadlines. Never pay someone who claims they can guarantee a scholarship decision.
