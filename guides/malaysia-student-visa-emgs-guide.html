Malaysia Student Visa Guide: EMGS, eVAL and Documents Explained

What happens after your offer letter and what to check before travelling.

Published 13 November 2026 · By NextGenz Centre Editorial Team

For international students, admission to a Malaysian university and permission to enter or remain in Malaysia are different processes. A university offer can be an important first step, but you should not assume it is your visa approval.

What are EMGS, a student pass and an eVAL?

Education Malaysia Global Services (EMGS) supports the international student pass application process. A student pass is an immigration pass for eligible international students. An electronic Visa Approval Letter (eVAL) is an approval issued before entry as part of that process. Depending on nationality, a separate Single Entry Visa may also be necessary.

The typical sequence

  1. Apply for and receive an offer from an approved education institution.
  2. Submit the applicable student pass application through the designated channel and provide the requested documents.
  3. Complete the assessment and respond promptly if supporting information is requested.
  4. Receive the relevant approval, including eVAL where applicable.
  5. Check whether you need a Single Entry Visa before travelling.
  6. Follow your institution's arrival, medical screening and student-pass endorsement instructions.

Documents to prepare

According to the official EMGS guidance, common documents include a compliant passport photograph, passport copies, an offer letter, academic certificates and transcripts, and a health declaration. Additional documents can depend on your nationality, programme or institution.

Check the current EMGS required-documents guidance rather than using an old social-media checklist. Passport validity and photograph requirements matter.

How long will it take?

Processing is not guaranteed on a fixed date. Document completeness, immigration checks and other agency decisions can affect the timeline. EMGS publishes its own processing guidance, and applications can require additional review. Build a buffer before the planned intake and confirm the latest status through official channels.

After arriving in Malaysia

New international students may need medical screening at an approved panel clinic within the applicable post-arrival period, followed by pass endorsement. Always check the current official timeline, your approval documents and your institution's instructions.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Booking non-refundable travel before the relevant approval.
  • Submitting unclear passport images or inconsistent names.
  • Assuming every nationality has identical entry requirements.
  • Confusing university application fees with student pass processing fees.
  • Relying on unofficial agents or promises of guaranteed approval.

For official details, use the EMGS visa portal. For help understanding admission and the document checklist, contact NextGenz Centre. Immigration decisions are made by the responsible authorities, not by education consultants.

Important: Requirements, fees and policies can change. Confirm the latest details with the institution and relevant authorities. Official reference ↗
