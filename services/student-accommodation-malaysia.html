BEFORE YOU BOOK

A few useful answers.

How do I receive the apartment photos and exact name?

Choose an NG reference and send it to our team on WhatsApp. We will confirm the relevant apartment details and gallery for your dates before you decide.

Do the displayed rates confirm availability?

No. They are indicative accommodation references from 22 November 2026. Availability, final dated price and additional charges need a fresh quotation.

Can I get a discount for my first weeks?

Ask our team to check a welcome offer. Eligible options, amount and conditions are confirmed in your individual quotation; an unconfirmed discount is not included in the displayed references.

Can my family stay with me?

Some configurations suit families or groups, subject to occupancy and operator rules. Share everyone’s needs so the beds, layout and guest count can be checked.

Can you help with my university application too?

Yes. NextGenz Centre also provides university placement and English-course admissions guidance. Accommodation charges are separate from our core free admissions advice.
