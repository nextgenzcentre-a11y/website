PRICING EXPLAINED

Is university placement really free?

Our core university placement advice is free for students. Where applicable, consultancy services may be supported by university recruitment or referral arrangements rather than student consultancy charges.

Free consultancy does not mean free education. Universities and authorities may charge tuition fees, deposits, registration fees, visa or EMGS-related fees, health insurance and other official costs. Confirm every charge with the institution before making a payment.

Important: Admissions, scholarship awards, discounts and student visa approvals are subject to separate decisions by institutions and authorities; none are guaranteed by NextGenz Centre.

QUESTIONS AND ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

Can international students apply to Malaysian universities with a free education consultant?

Yes. NextGenz Centre offers free core placement advisory and application guidance. Tuition and other official charges are separate.

Which universities and courses can I choose?

Options depend on your previous education, intended course, available intake and the university's current entry requirements. Start with your qualifications and preferred field so suitable options can be discussed.

Is IELTS always required to study in Malaysia?

No single rule applies to all programmes. Universities may accept different English qualifications or offer language pathways. Check the specific programme's current requirements before applying.

Can NextGenz Centre guarantee admission or a visa?

No. Universities decide admissions and the relevant authorities decide visa and student pass outcomes. We help you understand the process but cannot guarantee an outcome.

What expenses should students budget for?

Review tuition by semester, year or total programme, plus any registration fees, tax, student pass fees, insurance, housing and daily living expenses. Always confirm current quotations.
